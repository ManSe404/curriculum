## The problem

Marketing content work was spread across an assortment of tools and manual steps —
research in one place, drafting in another, publishing by hand at the end. The slow part
was never the writing. It was everything around it: finding what was current, getting it
in front of whoever was writing, and then moving the finished piece out to wherever it
needed to go.

## The approach

I built the platform end to end — a working application rather than a prototype:

- **Web search and deep-research agents**, gathering and synthesizing current source
  material instead of leaving a writer to open twenty tabs and reconcile them.
- **Agent-driven content generation**, drafting long-form pieces and blog posts from that
  research rather than from a blank page.
- **A content management core** for creating, organising, and versioning what gets
  produced, so output has somewhere to live between drafting and publishing.
- **Publishing integrations**, pushing finished content out to connected platforms rather
  than ending the workflow at a copy-paste step.

## The outcome

One system covering the whole path from research question to published piece. The parts
that were manual and repetitive — gathering sources, producing a first draft, distributing
the result — became things the platform does, which leaves the judgement calls to the
people who should be making them.
