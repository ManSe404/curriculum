Designed and built large-scale document ingestion and transformation pipelines on AWS,
structured as serverless workflows covering load, parse, transform, and S3 delivery.

Going serverless with Step Functions orchestration meant throughput scaled with demand
rather than with provisioned capacity — which is where the reliability gains and the
drop in operational overhead both came from.
