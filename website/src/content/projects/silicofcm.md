SILICOFCM is an EU Horizon 2020 computational platform for in silico clinical trials of
familial cardiomyopathies — running trials computationally, on models, where running them
on patients is impractical or unethical.

My contribution covered ECG and CPET analysis, statistical modelling, and work on
patient-specific 3D heart models. The output fed both the platform itself and the
peer-reviewed research around it, with the underlying aim of making personalized
cardiac medicine tractable.
