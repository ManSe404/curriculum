Delivered the reverse engineering and backend work on a project for one of Q Agency's
enterprise clients.

Reverse engineering came first: establishing what the existing system actually did, since
that is the prerequisite for replacing any part of it safely. Documented behaviour and
real behaviour diverge in systems that have been running for years, and the gaps are
where the risk sits. With the behaviour recovered and specified, I built out the backend
against it.
