Built an Azure-based extraction and transformation platform for analytics workflows,
orchestrating ETL end to end: raw ingestion, classification, embedding generation, and
reporting outputs.

The Power BI integration mattered as much as the pipeline — it turned the platform from
an engineering artifact into something business users could serve themselves from
without routing every question back through the data team.
